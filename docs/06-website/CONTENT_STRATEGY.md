# REVIA — Content Strategy

## 1. Mission

Create useful, understandable and clinically responsible content that helps people learn about rehabilitation and wellbeing.

## 2. Content Categories

### Rehabilitation

- Recovery education
- Mobility
- Strength
- Functional activities
- Exercise education

### Heart

- Cardiac recovery
- Lifestyle
- Activity
- Rehabilitation education

### Neuro

- Stroke recovery
- Paralysis
- Hand movement
- Walking
- Balance
- Speech
- Cognitive rehabilitation

### Kidney

- Renal rehabilitation
- Dialysis lifestyle education
- Fatigue
- Physical activity

### Liver

- Cirrhosis
- Muscle loss
- Mobility
- Nutrition education
- Functional recovery

### Wellbeing

- Sleep
- Mood
- Nutrition
- Stress
- Lifestyle
- Motivation

## 3. Content Types

- Articles
- Guides
- Short explainers
- Videos
- FAQs
- News
- Research summaries
- App feature announcements
- Newsletter editions

## 4. Clinical Content Workflow

```text
Research
 ↓
Draft
 ↓
Clinical Review
 ↓
Fact Check
 ↓
SEO Review
 ↓
Publish
 ↓
Periodic Review
```

## 5. News

News must clearly separate:

- Reported facts
- Interpretation
- REVIA commentary

Do not turn medical news into patient-specific advice.

## 6. Newsletter

Suggested cadence:

> Weekly or biweekly

Sections:

- Recovery insight
- Wellbeing tip
- New article
- New resource
- Product update

## 7. Tone

Use:

- Simple language
- Respectful language
- Encouragement
- Evidence-aware explanations

Avoid:

- Fear marketing
- Miracle claims
- Shame
- Excessive medical jargon

## 8. Localization

Start with:

- English
- Hindi
- Marathi

Use professional review for clinical translations.
