# NIRVAN — SEO Strategy

## 1. SEO Goal

Build NIRVAN as a trusted rehabilitation and wellbeing education brand.

SEO should target informational and discovery intent rather than making unsupported medical claims.

## 2. Core Keyword Clusters

### Cardiac

- cardiac rehabilitation
- cardiac recovery
- heart rehabilitation exercises
- cardiac rehab at home

### Neuro

- stroke rehabilitation
- stroke recovery exercises
- paralysis rehabilitation
- hand rehabilitation after stroke
- balance exercises after stroke

### Kidney

- kidney rehabilitation
- renal rehabilitation
- exercise and kidney disease
- dialysis exercise education

### Liver

- liver rehabilitation
- cirrhosis rehabilitation
- liver disease exercise
- muscle loss in cirrhosis

### General

- rehabilitation exercises
- recovery exercises
- home rehabilitation
- mobility exercises
- recovery wellbeing

## 4. Content Pillars

1. Rehabilitation
2. Conditions
3. Exercises
4. Wellbeing
5. Recovery journeys
6. Digital health
7. Research and evidence

## 5. E-E-A-T

Build trust through:

- Qualified reviewers
- Source references
- Review dates
- Author profiles
- Transparent disclaimers
- Accurate content
- Clear distinction between education and medical advice

## 6. Internal Linking

Every article should link to:

- Related condition
- Relevant exercise content
- Related wellbeing content
- App download
- Related articles

## 7. Avoid

Do not publish claims such as:

- Cure paralysis
- Reverse heart disease
- Cure kidney disease
- Reverse cirrhosis

unless a specific claim is scientifically and legally substantiated and appropriately reviewed.

## 8. Technical SEO

Implement:

- XML sitemap
- robots.txt
- Canonicals
- Metadata
- Open Graph
- JSON-LD
- Breadcrumbs
- 404 page
- Redirect strategy

## 9. Measurement

Track:

- Organic sessions
- Search impressions
- Click-through rate
- Ranking trends
- Landing-page conversions
- Newsletter subscriptions
- App download clicks
