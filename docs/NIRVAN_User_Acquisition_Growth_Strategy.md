# NIRVAN — User Acquisition & Growth Strategy

> **Move Forward. Live Better.**

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the user acquisition strategy for NIRVAN in India.

NIRVAN is a patient-focused rehabilitation and wellbeing app covering:

- ❤️ Heart / Cardiac Recovery
- 🧠 Neuro / Stroke / Paralysis Recovery
- 🫘 Kidney / Renal Recovery
- 🫀 Liver Recovery
- 🌿 Wellbeing

Initial app languages:

- English
- हिन्दी (Hindi)

The objective is to build sustainable acquisition through **trust, education, search, healthcare partnerships, referrals and useful recovery content** rather than relying primarily on paid advertising.

---

# 2. Core Growth Principle

NIRVAN should create native Hindi content rather than simply translating English articles.

Examples:

> **स्ट्रोक के बाद recovery कैसे करें?**

> **हार्ट अटैक के बाद exercise कब शुरू करें?**

# 44. Trust Strategy

Healthcare acquisition depends on trust.

NIRVAN should clearly communicate:

- Who creates the content
- Clinical review where applicable
- Content review dates
- Sources
- Privacy practices
- Health-data handling
- Limitations of the app
- When users should contact a healthcare professional

Avoid:

> Guaranteed recovery

> Cure paralysis

> Reverse heart disease

> AI doctor

> Guaranteed results

Prefer:

> Support your recovery journey.

> Learn about rehabilitation.

> Track your activities and progress.

> Follow the plan provided by your healthcare professional.

# 48. Final Strategy

NIRVAN should not attempt to win India through the largest advertising budget.

It should win through:

### 1. **Search**

Become discoverable when patients and families ask recovery questions.

### 2. **Education**

Become a trusted recovery knowledge platform.

### 3. **Healthcare professionals**

Build referral relationships with physiotherapists, rehabilitation centers and hospitals.

### 4. **Hindi**

Make recovery information accessible to Indian users in natural Hindi.

### 5. **YouTube**

Demonstrate recovery education visually.

### 6. **Family sharing**

Allow patients to share meaningful progress without requiring a caregiver app.

### 7. **Product experience**

Turn recovery into a simple, understandable journey.

### 8. **Referrals**

Let satisfied users introduce NIRVAN to others.
